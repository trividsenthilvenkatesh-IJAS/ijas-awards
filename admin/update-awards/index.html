Update Awards

The Awards page and the Project Database update automatically once a day (about 2 AM Central) from the IJAS Google Sheet. To change what the site shows, edit the sheet. No other steps are needed.

Open the awards Google Sheet (tabs "Awards 2026" and "Projects Database").

2026 projects appear on both tabs. You can edit either one: when the tabs disagree, the tab that was edited most recently wins (Awards 2026 if both were edited). 2025 projects come from Projects Database.

Need it updated right away? Open Update from Google Sheet on GitHub, press "Run workflow", then "Run workflow" again. The site refreshes within a few minutes. This button needs a GitHub account with access to the site repository.

If an update fails (for example the sheet can't be read or has far fewer projects than expected), the site keeps showing the last good version.
